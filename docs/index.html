002 · Who we are

A holding company
for the work that
actually has to ship.

We don't sell software. We don't sell hours. We sell the outcome: books that close on time, a site that earns calls, and an operating layer that does the boring work in the background.

Every brand under Turnkey ships against the same disciplines — clear scope, fixed cadence, transparent pricing, and an audit trail you can point at when the books are open. One founder is accountable for every engagement. No middle layer. No "let me check with the team."

The brands are priced as standalones, but they compound. CFO closes your month. WEB turns the inquiries into calls. AI runs the work between them.
