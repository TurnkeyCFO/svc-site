Principles

Four disciplines that hold across every brand we run.

— 01

Outcome over output.

Books that close on time. Sites that earn calls. Agents that finish the work. We don't sell hours and we don't sell software — we sell the result.

— 02

One founder accountable.

Every brand runs through Ricky. No account-manager middle layer. No "let me check with the team." If it ships under Turnkey, the founder is on the hook.

— 03

Audit trail by default.

Every action across CFO, WEB, and AI writes to a single discipline of source-of-truth ownership. You can point at what happened, when, and why — every time.

— 04

Boring on purpose.

The work that breaks small businesses isn't glamorous — it's the recurring, repetitive, error-prone stuff. We run toward it, systemize it, and put it on rails.

"Small businesses don't need another vendor. They need a partner who runs the boring work the same way every single month."
— Ricky West, Founder
