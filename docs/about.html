006 · Principles

Outcome over output.

Books that close on time. Sites that earn calls. Agents that finish the work. We don't sell hours and we don't sell software — we sell the result.

Every brand runs through Ricky. No account-manager middle layer. No "let me check with the team." If it ships under Turnkey, the founder is on the hook.
